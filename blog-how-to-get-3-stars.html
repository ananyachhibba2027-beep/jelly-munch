How to get 3 stars on every Jelly Munch level

By Ananya · 10 Nov 2026

Every level in Jelly Munch gives you up to 3 stars. Stars unlock new jelly skins and decide your spot on the leaderboard, so they're worth chasing. Here's how they work and how to get them.

How stars are counted

You get stars for how fast you finish, not how many hearts you keep. Every level shows a target time on the start screen ("Finish in under 0:19 for 3 stars"). Beat it for 3 stars, get close for 2, and just finishing gets you 1.

💡 You can replay any level you've cleared. Your best result is the one that counts.

1. Don't wait. Turn early.

Most lost time comes from going the long way round. As soon as the fruit appears, pick the shortest path and turn early. Don't run to the wall and then think.

2. Chain your munches for combos

If you eat the next fruit within a few seconds of the last one, you start a combo. It feels great and keeps you moving, which is exactly what fast times need.

3. Golden fruit counts double

From level 3 onwards, golden fruit sometimes appears. It's worth 2 and disappears after a few seconds, so go for it if it's close. Two golden fruits can save you a lot of time.

4. Use power-ups wisely

  • Magnet pulls nearby fruit to you. Great for speed.
  • Double munch makes every fruit count twice for a few seconds. Eat as many as you can while it lasts.
  • Slow-mo helps in tight mazes, but it slows your time too. Use it only when you need it.
  • Ghost lets you pass through walls and your tail. Use it to take shortcuts.

5. Keep your tail out of the way

As you grow, your tail becomes the main danger. Try to move in big loops and keep the middle of the board open, so you always have space to turn.

6. On a phone? Short swipes

Quick, short swipes work best. You can also use the arrow pad at the bottom of the screen. If the game feels too fast, try Chill mode from the home screen. It slows the snake down, and you can still earn stars.

7. Portals are shortcuts

In Ice Pop Lake, swimming into one portal pops you out of the other. Use them to cross the board in one move.
