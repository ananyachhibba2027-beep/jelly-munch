Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Jelly Munch ("we", "us") is a browser game at jellymunch.in. This page explains what data we collect when you play, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Game progress on your device. Your levels, stars, skins and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device unless you sign in.
  • Leaderboard. If you post a score, we store the nickname you choose, your score and a random player ID. Your nickname and score are shown publicly.
  • Optional account. If you sign in to save progress, we store your email address and your game progress so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases. If you buy levels or lives, payment is handled by Dodo Payments, which processes payments for us. We receive confirmation of the purchase and your email, but we never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Usage analytics. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people play: clicks, scrolling, device type, approximate location and session recordings. Clarity uses cookies to do this.

Advertising

Jelly Munch may show ads, including from Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and others.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses data from partner sites.

Who we share it with

We only share data with the services that run the game:

  • Supabase (leaderboard and accounts)
  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Dodo Payments (payments)
  • Microsoft Clarity (analytics)
  • Our ad partners

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep account and leaderboard data until you ask us to delete it. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing in.
  • You can clear local progress at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Children

Jelly Munch is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
