Jelly Munch daily challenge: how it works and how to win

By Ananya · 10 Nov 2026

Every day, Jelly Munch has a new daily challenge. It's a single mission that's the same for the whole day. Beat it and you win free lives and build a streak.

The rules

  • It's free. It doesn't use your lives.
  • You get 3 tries a day, and one heart per try, so every crash counts.
  • Win it for +2 lives and your 🔥 streak goes up.
  • A new challenge drops every day at midnight (India time).

The missions

⏱ Speed Run

Eat a set number of fruits before the clock hits zero. The clock counts down at the top. Tip: don't chase golden fruit unless it's on your way. Speed matters more than points here.

⭐ Golden Hunter

Only golden fruit counts. Eating normal fruit makes golden fruit appear, so keep munching and jump on the gold when it shows up. It fades after a few seconds.

🍫 Fudge Run

Clear the goal inside the fudge maze. The gaps between the walls are your roads. Plan your route before you go for each fruit.

🐸 Hop Chase

The fruit hops around every few seconds. Don't chase where it is, cut it off where it's going. Portals can help you get there first.

Keep the streak alive

Your streak grows every day you win, and resets if you miss a day. Even one quick win a day keeps it going. It's the best way to keep your lives topped up too.
